## Baghdad FIR
This section covers the positions within the Baghdad (ORBB) FIR.

En-route control for Baghdad will only receive aircraft on the following routes:

| ADEP | ADES | Route Segment               | Entry | Exit |
| ---- | ---- | --------------------------- | ----- | ---- |
| OMDB | LKPR | TASMI L602 GADSI M860 NINVA | 1119  | 1611 | 
| OKKK | LKPR | TASMI L602 GADSI M860 NINVA | 1125  | 1515 | 
| OKKK | LHBP | TASMI L602 ALPET L718 KABAN | 1125  | 1515 | 
| OKKK | ESSA | TASMI L602 ALPET L718 KABAN | 1125  | 1515 | 

Sectorisation and extra information for the Baghdad FIR can be found [here](http://127.0.0.1:8000/Baghdad%20FIR/Enroute/Baghdad%20Control/ "Opens in new tab"){target="_blank"}.

### Baghdad Control North (ORBB_N_CTR)
Aircraft will be handed off by Baghdad Control South (BS) and should be at RFL, or climbing to RFL. <br> Aircraft should be handed off ~20nm prior to the sector boundary.

A 'procedural' area exists in the west of the sector. More information can be found in the [Procedural page](https://docs.kuwait-iraqvacc.net/Baghdad%20FIR/Enroute/Procedural/ "Opens in new tab"){target="_blank"}.

Baghdad Control North provides top-down services for the following airports:

- ORBI, ORER, ORKK, ORSJ

Airports within the Kirkuk TMA sector (all, excluding ORBI) have standard procedures, though note that radar vectoring is preferred.

A common transition altitude of 14 000 ft and a common transition level of FL 160, which increases to FL 170 when QNH is below 980 hPa, are established for the whole of the Baghdad FIR.

### Baghdad Control South (ORBB_S_CTR)
If online, ALI_APP will be handing event aircraft to you climbing FL230. Baghdad Control should climb them further to their RFL. <br> Aircraft should be handed off ~20nm prior to the sector boundary.

Baghdad Control South provides top-down services for the following airports:

- ORNI, ORMM

Only ORNI has standard procedures, which should be used to the most extent.

A common transition altitude of 14 000 ft and a common transition level of FL 160, which increases to FL 170 when QNH is below 980 hPa, are established for the whole of the Baghdad FIR.

### Ali TMA Sector (ALI_APP)
Aircraft will be handed off by OKKK_APP at COPN TASMI climbing FL160. Lower levels may be coordinated.

Aircraft should be handed off to ORBB_S_CTR climbing FL230. Controllers should make the best attempt to handoff aircraft at the earliest possible opportunity.

Sectorisation for the Ali TMA Sector - and surrounding TMA sectors - can be found [here](https://docs.kuwait-iraqvacc.net/Baghdad%20FIR/Terminal/ALI/ "Opens in new tab"){target="_blank"}.

A common transition altitude of 14 000 ft and a common transition level of FL 160, which increases to FL 170 when QNH is below 980 hPa, are established for the whole of the Baghdad FIR.

### Kuwait Aerodrome

#### Kuwait Tower
Kuwait Tower should make their best effort to get departures out at the best possible rate whilst not compromising separation standards. All departures will be on RW15R/RW33L and sent to the standard holding points (W2 for Medium WTC aircraft).

Kuwait Tower should utilise the CDM plugin (Combined Taxi Out List) to depart aircraft according to their slot (CTOT).

#### Kuwait Ground
Kuwait Ground will be bandboxing both GMC positions, East and West.

Standard Taxi Routes should be used as per [PRC-OKKK-26-001](https://docs.kuwait-iraqvacc.net/Controller%20Guidance/Procedure%20Updates/Updates/2026/10/20/prc-okkk-26-001--introduction-of-standard-taxi-routes-to-kuwait-okkk/ Opens in new tab"){target="_blank"}.

If using RW33L, all Medium (M) WTC aircraft should be taxied to holding point W2 via standard routing: "KAC123, taxi to holding point W2 runway 33L via standard routing"

Ground should make the best effort to sequence aircraft to the TWR controller for the best possible departure order. Taxiways W3 and W19 may be used for this.

#### Kuwait Delivery
CDM must be used at all times, and VATCAN plugin to check the aircraft's slot. The VATCAN code is `XhjCw`

DCL must be used at all times.

Non-event aircraft may be given ATC clearance, and CDM guidance to be used for start clearance. If CDM is not working, start at own risk.

If GMC-W is online, ensure that aircraft are being handed off to the correct controller for start.
