## Summary

From the effective date, controllers on all Kuwait-Iraq vACC positions in the Kuwait FIR and the Baghdad FIR **must be available for coordination on vacs or Discord**. Being available on **both** is preferred. vacs was previously only strongly recommended.

This applies to all coordination with adjacent KWIQ positions, including departure releases, handoff coordination and any non-standard requests.

## Coordination Methods

**EuroScope's built-in coordination functions (COPX, COPN, PEL, XFL) are always the preferred method**, whoever the adjacent controller is. Use voice or text coordination only when the built-in functions can't handle the request.

!!! note "Neighbouring vACCs"
    Neighbouring vACCs may not be using vacs. When coordinating across the vACC boundary, use the following, in order of preference:

    1. EuroScope built-in coordination functions (COPX, COPN, PEL, XFL)
    2. EuroScope private chat
    3. Any other agreed means

## Controller Actions

- Whenever you are logged on to a KWIQ position, be available on vacs or in the Kuwait & Iraq vACC Discord server. Both is preferred.
- Use EuroScope built-in coordination functions wherever possible.
- For coordination the built-in functions can't handle, use vacs or Discord with adjacent KWIQ controllers.
- With neighbouring vACCs, use built-in functions, then EuroScope private chat or other means if they are not on vacs.
