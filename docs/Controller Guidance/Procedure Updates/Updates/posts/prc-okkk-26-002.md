## Summary

From **AIRAC 2611 (29/10/2026)**, the logon callsigns for the two Kuwait Ground (GMC) positions change to reflect the side of the aerodrome they control.

| Position | Old Logon Callsign | New Logon Callsign | Frequency |
| -------- | ------------------ | ------------------ | --------- |
| **GMC-E** | ~~OKKK_1_GND~~    | **OKKK_E_GND**     | 121.700   |
| **GMC-W** | ~~OKKK_2_GND~~    | **OKKK_W_GND**     | 121.850   |

The radio callsign (**Kuwait Ground**), frequencies and areas of responsibility are **unchanged**.

## Controller Actions

- From 29/10/2026, log on using the new callsigns only.
- **GMC-E (OKKK_E_GND)** remains the primary GMC position and bandboxes DEL and GMC-W when they are offline.
- **GMC-W (OKKK_W_GND)** must still **not** be opened without DEL being online first.

## Documentation Status

The Kuwait Ground document, OKKK General Information and the OKKK Pilot Briefing have been updated to reflect this change.
