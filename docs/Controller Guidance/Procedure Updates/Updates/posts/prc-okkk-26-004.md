## Summary

The OKKK documentation has been aligned with the Kuwait AIP supplements currently in force. Three supplements change how OKKK is controlled.

| Supplement           | In force                    | Change                                              |
| -------------------- | --------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| **AIRAC AIP SUP 04/26** | From 01/10/2026          | New SIDs **TASMI1K** and **TASMI5E**                 |
| **AIP SUP 01/26**    | 06/01/2026 – 22/02/2027     | **RWY 15L/33R closed**                               |
| **AIP SUP 02/26**    | From 09/06/2026             | **TWY E1** restricted for Code D, E and F aircraft   |

## SIDs (AIRAC AIP SUP 04/26)

All OKKK SID, STAR, ILS and RNP charts have been replaced. For SID allocation, two SIDs change:

| Runway  | Withdrawn          | Replaced by        |
| ------- | ------------------ | ------------------ |
| **16**  | ~~RALKA1K~~        | **TASMI1K**        |
| **15R** | ~~RALKA5E~~        | **TASMI5E**        |

Departures towards TASMI now have a TASMI SID from every runway (TASMI1K, TASMI5E, TASMI5F and TASMI1I). Speed restrictions have also been removed from all SIDs.

!!! info "Sector files and plugins"
    The **vSID** plugin configuration and the **OKAC sector files** have been updated to reflect these SID changes. Ensure you are using the latest sector file package.

!!! warning
    Do not assign RALKA1K or RALKA5E. Pilots using an older AIRAC may not have the TASMI SIDs for RWY 16 and RWY 15R.

## RWY 15L/33R closed (AIP SUP 01/26)

RWY 15L/33R is **closed to all aircraft operations** for rehabilitation until **22/02/2027**. The associated ILS has been removed and its charts withdrawn. The following are also closed:

- TWY E2 (affected portions)
- RWY 15L loops (Main Loop 1 and Loop 2)
- RWY 33R loops (Main Loop 1 and Loop 2)

Only RWY 15R/33L and RWY 16/34 are available.

## TWY E1 (AIP SUP 02/26)

TWY E1 is partially restricted due to construction works for the new RWY 15L/33R.

- **Code D, E and F** aircraft taxiing to or from the Eastern Apron, Amiri Terminal or VIP Terminal shall use **TWY E10**, escorted by a **follow-me** vehicle.
- **Code D, E and F** aircraft taxiing out of the G/A Terminal northbound via E1 shall vacate E1 via **E8** only. Those taxiing in southbound shall use **E8** only.
- **Code C and smaller** aircraft may use E1 or E10.

!!! note "Coordination"
    **GMC** should expect more taxi delays and should plan routings for larger aircraft from the Eastern Apron early.

## Documentation Status

The Kuwait Delivery, Kuwait Ground and Kuwait Tower documents and OKKK General Information have been updated to reflect these supplements.
