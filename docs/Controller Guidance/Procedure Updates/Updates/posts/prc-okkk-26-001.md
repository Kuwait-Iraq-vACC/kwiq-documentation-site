## Summary

Standard Taxi Routes (STR) have been introduced at Kuwait International Airport (OKKK) following the real world change ([AIRAC AIP SUP 03/26, effective 03 Sep 2026](https://cdn.kuwait-iraqvacc.net/Operations/Controllers/STR%20AIRAC%20AIP%20SUP%20all.pdf "Opens in a new tab"){target="_blank"})).

STRs are published for departures and arrivals on **RWY 15R/16** and **RWY 33L/34**, and are referenced in taxi clearances using the phrase **"Via Standard Routing"**. Where an STR is published for the aircraft's stand and runway, **GMC** should use it.

!!! note "Real world supplement"
The supplement states that aircraft taxi in accordance with the published STR unless ATC instructs otherwise, and that ATC may assign an alternative route at any time for operational, traffic, weather, construction, emergency or safety reasons.

The charts can be found at [vats.im/okkkSTR](https://vats.im/okkkSTR "Opens in a new tab"){target="_blank"}

## Standard taxi clearances

| Phase                | Phraseology                                                         |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Departure (full length)**  | KAC343, taxi to holding point RWY15R via Standard Routing, QNH 1013 |
| **Departure (intersection)**  | KAC343, taxi to holding point W2 RWY33L via Standard Routing, QNH 1013 |
| **Arrival**          | KAC418, taxi to stand 72 via Standard Routing                       |

!!! warning
The STR depends on the aircraft's stand and the runway in use. Always check the chart for the correct runway before issuing "Via Standard Routing".

!!! tip "Progressive taxi"
Where no STR is published, or the traffic situation requires it, use standard taxi instructions as before. Progressive taxi instructions also remain available on pilot request.

## Runway crossings

"Via Standard Routing" **never** includes a runway crossing clearance. Where an STR crosses RWY 15R/33L, the aircraft must hold short until **TWR** issues an explicit crossing clearance, and the crew must read it back.

!!! note "Coordination"
**GMC** should coordinate with **TWR** early for any aircraft whose STR crosses RWY 15R/33L.

## TWR operations

- Runway crossing clearances for RWY 15R/33L are issued by **TWR**.
- Vacating aircraft continue to receive their initial taxi instruction from **TWR** before transfer. **GMC** then issues the arrival STR to the stand.

## Low Visibility Procedures

STRs remain in force during Low Visibility Procedures unless otherwise instructed. **GMC** may assign an alternative route where necessary to keep traffic flowing safely.

## Documentation Status

The OKKK Pilot Briefing has been amended to reflect this change. The Kuwait Ground (GND) document has not been updated yet: sections 2.2.1 and 2.2.2 still describe the previous taxi routings and diagrams. Until it is, this instruction takes precedence.
