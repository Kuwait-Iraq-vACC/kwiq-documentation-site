## Summary

Iraq AIP Amendment 11/26 takes effect with AIRAC 2612. It withdraws the RNP approaches to runways 18 and 36 at Erbil. Only the ILS/DME Y and Z approaches remain.

## Controller Actions

- **Don't clear aircraft for an RNP approach at ORER.** Use the ILS/DME Y or Z approach instead.

## Documentation Status

Documentation changes not required.

The RNP approaches were removed from the TopSky CFL menu in the AIRAC 2612 release.
