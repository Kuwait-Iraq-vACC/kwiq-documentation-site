## General Information
**ICAO/IATA** OKKK/KWI <br> **Elevation** 204 ft <br> **Transition Altitude** 13000 ft <br> **Transition Level** FL150
### Navigation Data
Since AIRAC 2511, major changes to the Kuwait Airport and its procedures have changed. Ensure that you have the most up-to-date navigational data and charts before flying.
