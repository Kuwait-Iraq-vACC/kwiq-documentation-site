## General Information

**ICAO / IATA:** OKKK / KWI  
**Elevation:** 204 ft  
**Transition Altitude:** 13,000 ft  
**Transition Level:** FL150

Kuwait International Airport (formerly OKBK) is Kuwait's only civil airport and is located in Farwaniya, approximately 8 NM south of Kuwait City. It serves as the primary hub for Kuwait Airways and Jazeera Airways.

### Navigation Data

Since AIRAC 2511, significant changes have been introduced to Kuwait's airport layout and procedures. Pilots must ensure they are using the latest navigation data and charts before operating to or from the aerodrome.

## Controller Positions

When flying to Kuwait, pilots should expect the following ATC positions:

| Callsign | Logon Callsign | Frequency |
| -------- | -------------- | --------- |
| Kuwait Information | `OKKK_ATIS` | 126.225 |
| Kuwait Delivery | `OKKK_DEL` | 126.775 |
| Kuwait Ground | `OKKK_1_GND` | 121.700 |
| Kuwait Ground | `OKKK_2_GND` | 121.850 |
| Kuwait Tower | `OKKK_TWR` | 118.300 |
| Kuwait Director | `OKKK_F_APP` | 123.825 |
| Kuwait Approach | `OKKK_APP` | 124.800 |

!!! info "Important"
    Auto handoffs are not used at Kuwait. Do not change frequencies unless explicitly instructed by ATC.  
    If you are unsure whether you are on the correct frequency, ask.

## Departure Procedures

### Clearance

!!! info "Information"
    Pilots should request clearance approximately 10 minutes prior to startup.

The preferred method of obtaining clearance is via PDC. In most cases, a PDC can be requested through the Hoppie network. Alternatively, a controller may send the PDC directly to the pilot client.

!!! note
    PDCs do not require a readback.

Voice clearances are also available. When requesting one, pilots must provide:

- Aircraft type
- Stand number
- ATIS letter

!!! example
    Kuwait Delivery, KAC673, A330, stand 79, information Golf, request clearance to Dubai

!!! warning
    Pilots must always report themselves as "fully ready" to ATC. Do not change frequencies without explicit instruction.  
    "Fully ready" means the aircraft is ready to push and move immediately when instructed.

#### Flow Initiatives

When traffic levels are high, or flow restrictions are in place, delays may be introduced. Pilots are expected to comply with any restrictions issued by ATC. Current restrictions can be reviewed at [vats.im](https://vats.im).

### Pushback

> Pilots should never change frequency automatically without explicit instruction.

!!! warning
    Ensure the pushback tag is connected and that the aircraft is fully ready before calling for push.

Pushback instructions at Kuwait follow standard procedures used at other airports. Controllers commonly instruct pilots to face a specific direction (north, east, south, or west), and may also include additional restrictions.

!!! example
    KAC303, push and start approved face south on W1, runway 33L

Pilots may also be instructed to remain clear of taxiways.

!!! example
    QTR1083, push and start approved face north, stay clear of taxiway W14, runway 33L

### Taxi Out

Kuwait uses Standard Taxi Routes (STR). These routes are published graphically in the relevant chart and are referenced in ATC taxi clearances by the phrase: "via standard routing".

!!! example
    ETD654, taxi to holding point runway 33L via standard routing

ATC may occasionally choose not to use an STR, in which case a standard taxi clearance will be issued.

!!! example
    JZR131, taxi to holding point runway 33L via W17, W12, W13, W9, W20, W19, W3, W1, W2

Charts for STRs can be found [here](https://cdn.kuwait-iraqvacc.net/Operations/Pilots/Kuwait/STR%20AIRAC%20AIP%20SUP%20Charts.pdf){target="_blank"}.

!!! warning
    Runway crossing clearances are always issued separately by ATC. Aircraft must not cross any runway unless an explicit runway crossing clearance has been received.

Depending on aircraft position, taxi clearances may be lengthy. If unsure, always ask ATC.

!!! note
    The standard holding point for Medium (M) wake turbulence category aircraft is W2.  
    The TORA from W2 is 2500 m.

### Takeoff

As soon as instructed by the ground controller, change to the assigned tower frequency to minimise delay for yourself and other aircraft.

When the pilot is given a runway clearance, they are expected to be ready for takeoff. If not ready, inform the tower controller as soon as possible.

After departure, no extra call to the tower controller is required. The controller will keep the aircraft on frequency until transfer is required.

### After Departure

!!! warning
    Unless otherwise instructed, all departures shall climb via SID to 4,000 ft.

After transfer by the tower controller, pilots are expected to change immediately to the assigned frequency. On first contact, pilots should provide:

- Callsign
- SID
- Passing altitude
- Cleared altitude

!!! example
    Kuwait Approach, KAC135, TASMI5F passing 2400, climbing 4000

If given an instruction, pilots should comply and then respond. A turn may be commenced while the controller is issuing instructions.
