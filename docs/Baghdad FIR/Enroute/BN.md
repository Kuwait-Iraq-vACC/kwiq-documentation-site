## Position Overview

| Radio Callsign  | Logon Callsign | Frequency | Levels (FL) | Class |
|-----------------|----------------|-----------|-------------|:-----:|
| Baghdad Control | ORBB_N_CTR     | 132.875   | 235 - 345   |   A   |

**Baghdad Control North Low (BN)** covers the North sector of the Baghdad FIR from FL235 to FL345. If **BNU** is not online, BN also covers North High (FL345 - FL460).

The North sector lies to the north and west of the line **VAXEN - SEPTU - DEBNI - BABIL - ANBAR**. It contains the procedural area in the west of the FIR. See the [Procedural page](Procedural.md).

## Top-down Services

When online, and the relevant approach and aerodrome positions are not, BN provides top-down services for:

- ORBI, ORBM, ORER, ORKK, ORSJ

Airports within the Kirkuk TMA (all, excluding ORBI) have standard procedures, though radar vectoring is preferred.

## Coordination

!!! warning "To be confirmed"
    Handoff and agreed levels are provisional and may change.

Aircraft will be handed off by **Baghdad Control South (BS)** at RFL, or climbing to RFL. Aircraft should be handed off **~20 NM** prior to the sector boundary.

Departures from the Baghdad and Kirkuk TMAs will be handed off by **BGD_APP** and **KRK_APP** climbing **FL230**.

Arrivals into ORBI from the north should cross **SOBIL at FL350**. See the provisional [agreed levels for ORBI arrivals](../Terminal/BGD.md#agreed-levels-orbi-arrivals).
