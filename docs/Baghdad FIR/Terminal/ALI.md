The **Ali Sector** covers the south of the Baghdad FIR, including Al Najaf (ORNI) and Basrah (ORMM), from the lower limits below up to **FL235**.

## Al Najaf (ORNI)

- The preferential runway for **landing is RWY 28**, and for **take-off is RWY 10**, unless tailwind conditions exist.
- Flying over the **Holy Shrine** is prohibited. Aircraft shall remain at least **2 NM** clear of the prohibited area (OR/P 102).
- When landing **RWY 10**, aircraft on right downwind shall make a short approach. If unable, aircraft shall extend downwind and not turn right before **8 DME ALI**.
- Aircraft departing **RWY 28** shall turn left immediately, passing 500ft and before 1000ft.

Aircraft arriving or departing ORNI are permitted to fly within restricted area **OR/R 103** (10 NM around the shrine, SFC - FL230).

## Basrah (ORMM)

- Aircraft inbound to Basrah from the **Kuwait FIR** are to call Baghdad Approach by **TASMI**.
- Route **G795** (TASMI - BSR) is used for traffic between Kuwait and Basrah, between **8000ft and 14000ft**.
- Omnidirectional departures climb on runway track to **600ft** before proceeding on course.
- Spiral climbs and descents are **not permitted**.

## Coordination

Departures from Kuwait (OKKK) will be handed off by **OKKK_APP** at **TASMI**, climbing to the level coordinated in the LoA. Departures should be handed off to **Baghdad Control South** climbing **FL230**.
