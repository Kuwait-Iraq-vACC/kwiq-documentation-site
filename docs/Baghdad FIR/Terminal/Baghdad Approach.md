title: "Baghdad Approach"
---

Divided into 3 different TMAs, Baghdad Approach provides approach services below FL235 throughout the whole of the Baghdad FIR.

Baghdad Approach ensures surveillance-based separation to aircraft departing, arriving, or transiting the Ali, Baghdad, or Kirkuk TMA sectors. This section outlines the procedures to ensure a safe and efficient flow when operating a Baghdad Approach position.

The following table dictates which airports each approach unit provides top-down ATC service for.

| Logon Callsign | Frequency | Sector                   | Aerodromes             |
| -------------- | --------- |--------------------------| ---------------------- |
| ALI_APP        | 120.200   | [Ali Sector](ALI.md)     | ORMM, ORNI             |
| BGD_APP        | 122.400   | [Baghdad Sector](BGD.md) | ORBI                   |
| KIR_APP        | 127.700   | [Kirkuk Sector](KIR.md)  | ORBM, ORER, ORKK, ORSJ |

All three sectors use the radio callsign **Baghdad Approach**.

## Airspace

All TMAs within the Baghdad FIR are **Class D** airspace, with an upper limit of **FL235**. Above FL235 is Class A airspace controlled by [Baghdad Control](../Enroute/Baghdad%20Control.md).

Lower limits vary for each part of the TMA. These are listed on each sector page. Where a lower limit is given as an altitude AMSL or a height AGL, **whichever is higher** applies.

ATS routes outside the TMAs and below FL235 are **Class E**. All other airspace is **Class G**.

### Transition Altitude and Level

| **Transition Altitude** | **Transition Level**                   |
|:-----------------------:|:--------------------------------------:|
|         14 000 ft        | FL160 (FL170 when QNH is below 980 hPa) |

The transition layer exists between these two levels and is **not to be used** for level flight.

## General Procedures

### Arrivals
IFR flights entering a TMA will be cleared to a reporting point and instructed to contact approach. If the clearance limit is reached before further instructions are received, aircraft will hold at the last authorised level.

An IFR aircraft not under a radar service, and not on a published RNAV arrival or a visual approach, must not descend below the MSA until over the IAF.

#### Visual Approaches
An arriving aircraft may be cleared for a visual approach provided:

- The pilot has, and can maintain, continuous visual reference to the ground; and
- At night, the pilot reports the aerodrome in sight; and
- VMC exists at the destination, or the pilot reports that a visual approach and landing can be completed.

Separation shall be maintained between IFR aircraft on a visual approach and other IFR aircraft. For successive visual approaches, radar separation is maintained until the following aircraft reports the preceding aircraft in sight.

### Departures
Where a SID has been assigned, an amended clearance shall **not** be issued while the aircraft is below the MSA/MVA, unless requested by the pilot. If requested, the pilot shall be advised that they are below the MSA/MVA:

> Controller: "IAW123, you are below the minimum vectoring altitude, turn left heading 270, maintain own terrain clearance to 6000ft"

### Holding
There are no published enroute holds in the Baghdad FIR. If required, aircraft may be instructed to hold at an enroute reporting point, as published or as instructed by ATC.

## Coordination

Departures should be handed off to [Baghdad Control](../Enroute/Baghdad%20Control.md) climbing **FL230**, at the earliest possible opportunity.

Arrivals will be handed off by Baghdad Control descending to **FL240** or above, unless otherwise coordinated.
