`*` Baghdad Director (FIN) is an events only position.

The **Baghdad Sector** covers central and western Iraq, including Baghdad International (ORBI), from the lower limits below up to **FL235**.

## Baghdad International (ORBI)

- No manoeuvres involving flight to the **east of the circuit** at ORBI should be made unless specifically cleared by ATC.
- Civil aircraft are not permitted to arrive or depart when the visibility is less than **800 m** or RVR **550 m**.
- IFR departures contact Ground at least **10 minutes** before pushback to receive their route clearance.

Training areas **SW1** to **SW10** lie south-east of ORBI. Aircraft must contact Baghdad Approach before entering, and contact ATC after departing ORSW.
