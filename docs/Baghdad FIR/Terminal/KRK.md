The **Kirkuk Sector** covers northern Iraq, including Kirkuk (ORKK), Erbil (ORER), Jalal Talabani (ORSJ) and Mosul (ORBM), from the lower limits below up to **FL235**.

The terrain in the north and east of the sector is significantly higher than the rest of the FIR, which is reflected in the higher lower limits of the TMA.

## Aerodrome Notes

### Erbil (ORER)
- **RWY 18** is the preferential runway, up to **8 kt** of tailwind.
- Aircraft shall remain clear of prohibited areas **OR/P 602** and **OR/P 603** (SFC - 7500ft) near the aerodrome.

### Jalal Talabani (ORSJ)
- All arriving and transiting aircraft are to contact Jalal Talabani Tower **prior to entering the CTR**.

### Kirkuk (ORKK) and Mosul (ORBM)
- Civil aircraft must notify ATC if unable to operate VFR below **3000ft** (Kirkuk) or **12000ft** (Mosul), using the phrase **"UNABLE VFR"**.
